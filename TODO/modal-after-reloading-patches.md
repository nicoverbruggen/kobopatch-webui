# Present a modal after reloading patches

When you are trying to patch your device w/ patches then the manifest can be loaded, and you are offered the chance to reload the patches to re-apply them.

When you choose to reload them, you should see a modal that says what the compatibility is of the changes vs. the current firmware. If a certain patch no longer works, it should be noted here (or: "all of the previously applied patches are compatible"). If the user customized the patch, note that it was customized and will be applied as-is.
