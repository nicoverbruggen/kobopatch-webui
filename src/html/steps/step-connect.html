Important information
If you choose to modify your Kobo's system files via this tool, your warranty may be affected. A backup of any extra ("sideloaded") books is recommended. If something goes wrong, follow the official instructions on how to manually reset your device.

How do you want to start?

Unlike some other e-readers, Kobo devices don't need a jailbreak for you to be able to install modifications on your device. All it takes is copying some files, but it can be a little difficult for non-technical people. I hope this site makes it easier.

Note: Your browser does not support direct device access via the native filesystem API, which is required for a seamless patching experience. Directly connecting to your Kobo is not available because of this. Use Chrome, Edge or Opera to connect your Kobo directly, or choose the manual download option.
