Connect your Kobo

Your Kobo's model and version number can be automatically identified. Based on this information, certain options will be presented to you as part of the next step.

  1. Connect your Kobo to this computer using a USB cable.
  2. Your Kobo will show a "Computer detected" screen. Tap Connect to continue.
  3. In your file manager, you should now see KOBOeReader appear as a drive.
  4. When you press the button below, your browser will open a folder picker. Select the KOBOeReader volume, then click "Allow" when your browser asks if you want to give this site permission to write to this folder.
Anything else I should know?
  1. Information about your device is never collected. It is only displayed on this page, temporarily, and used to determine which options are compatible with your device.
  2. This website will only write to your device when you press the "Write to Kobo" button at the end of the wizard. You also have the option to download an archive instead.
