Something went wrong

Some patches may not work correctly with your software version. You can go back and try a different selection.


                

                    
This is almost always a problem with the USB connection between your computer and your Kobo, not with the patches or options you picked.

                    
    
                        
  1. Reconnect your Kobo. Safely eject the Kobo, unplug the cable, turn the Kobo off and on again, then plug it back in and try once more.
    2. 
                        
  2. Try a different cable or port. Some USB cables can only charge and can’t transfer files. Use another cable, and try a different USB port on your computer.
    3. 
                        
  3. Copy the files yourself instead. Use “Start Over”, then pick “Build downloadable archive” on the first screen to save a ZIP file. Carefully follow the included instructions to copy its contents onto your Kobo. Following each step closely is important to avoid damaging your device.
    4. 
                    

                    
Still stuck, or your Kobo is misbehaving? A factory reset returns it to its original out-of-the-box state. Treat this as a last resort: it erases the books and settings stored on the device, so make sure your backup is OK before doing this.
