Since you have chosen not to connect your device, you will need to look up the software version and firmware channel for the device you want to patch.

Select the version number currently installed on your device. If it does not appear in this list, your software version is not supported for custom patching. You can go back and choose NickelMenu instead, which works with all versions.

You can find the version number on your Kobo under More > Settings > Device information > Software version. Make sure it's a complete match, or you may encounter issues!

Select the firmware channel that matches your device family and first few digits of your serial number. If you are not sure, connect your Kobo directly using a compatible browser so the hardware UUID can be checked automatically.
