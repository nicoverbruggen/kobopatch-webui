Before continuing, choose whether you want to create a local backup from your Kobo first. This happens entirely in your browser on this computer and is never uploaded to any server.

A backup of your files is only created locally via your browser. Your backup is never uploaded anywhere. Once ready, your browser will "download" an archive that you can use to restore files. Store this archive in a safe location, in the unlikely event that something goes wrong.

  1. Connect your Kobo via USB so it appears as a removable drive.
  2. Copy the hidden .kobo folder to a safe folder on your computer.
  3. When the files have been copied, you can continue with the wizard. Make sure to store this copy in a safe location, in the unlikely event something goes wrong.
